# Card Conjurer (cardconjurer.app): Full Options Inventory

Collected November 1, 2026 (381 distinct frame packs across 15 groups) from the live creator at https://cardconjurer.app/creator/ (no login, nothing saved, downloaded or shared).

**Correction to my earlier file:** that one covered cardconjurer.com, a different site, and wrongly said there were no Magic frames. This is the right one. The Magic frames sit in the **Frame** tab, under the Frame Group and Frame Pack dropdowns.

How this was collected: I read the page's own dropdowns and its in-page help text, then stepped through every Frame Pack and read the frame images it loads. Counts in parentheses are the number of frame images in that pack. Color variants are collapsed into one line.

**Color key used below:** W white, U blue, B black, R red, G green, M multicolor/gold, A artifact, L land, C colorless, V vehicle, E Eldrazi, Au gold, Ag silver, Gy gray, Pu purple, Br brown. "◇ Frame [WUBRGMA]" means one frame image per listed color. "W+L" means a white-and-land hybrid look.

---

## 1. Page layout

Tabs along the top of the editor: **Frame, Text, Art, Set Symbol, Watermark, Collector, Import/Save, Tutorial.** A **Download your card** button sits under the card preview. There are also two pop-up editors: **Frame Image Editor** and **Textbox Editor**.

---

## 2. Frame tab: groups and packs

Pick a **Frame Group**, then a **Frame Pack** (or type in the **Search Frames...** box). Press **Load Frame Version** to configure text placement, art size and so on for that pack (a checkbox "Automatically load Frame Version when loading Frame Packs" does this for you). Then add frame images to the card.

**Frame Groups (15 usable, under four headings):**
- *Standard Frames:* Regular, Tokens, Sagas, Planeswalkers, Modal DFC's, Transform
- *Special Frames:* Showcase Frames, Universes Beyond, Promos (Tall Art), Textless/Fullart
- *Other Frames:* Custom, Old/Misc, Accurate Frames, 1/8th Inch Margin
- *Other Games:* Flesh and Blood

**Adding frames:** double-click a frame or mask to add it. Holding Shift / Ctrl / Alt while double-clicking adds it to the right half / left half / middle third. Buttons: Add Frame to Card, Right Half, Left Half, Middle Third, Top Half, Bottom Half. A **"No Mask"** option sits beside the mask picker; the Regular pack's masks are Pinline, Title, Type, Rules, Frame and Border. Frames can be dragged to reorder, and clicked to edit opacity, position and size. You can upload custom frame images by drag and drop.

**Other Frame tab options:** Rounded Corners (when downloaded), Guidelines (for text, art, watermark and set symbol), Transparencies highlight.

**Frame Image Editor** (pop-up): X, Y, Width, Height, Opacity, Erase, Erase Card, Blending Mode, Preserve Alpha, Color Overlay, HSL Adjustments, and a list of masks to select and remove or drag and drop to add.

**Auto-frame dropdown (26 entries):** Disabled, Regular, Extended Art, Extended Art (Shorter Textbox), Universes Beyond, Etched, Borderless (Alt), Borderless (Alt) (Universes Beyond), Phyrexian, Eighth Edition, Seventh Edition, Japan Showcase, Vault (BIG), Adventure, Omen, Prepare, Regular (Accurate), Full art (Accurate), Universes Beyond (Accurate), Circuit, M15-Eighth, M15-Eighth Universes Beyond.

## 4. Text tab

**Text areas to edit:** Mana Cost, Title, Type, Rules Text, Power/Toughness. The page also has an **"Add a textbox to your card"** section; the labels Nickname, Power/Toughness and Date Stamp appear next to it, but I did not test what each adds.

**Per-textbox controls:** a text field per area, **Italic** and **Bold** buttons, **Edit Bounds** (move and resize the box), and **Adjust font size**. Two options for reminder text: **Hide reminder text** and **Auto-italicize reminder text**.

### Text codes
| Code | Result |
|---|---|
| `{cardname}` or `~` | Name of the card |
| `{-}` | Em-dash |
| `{i}` `{/i}` | Start/stop italics |
| `{bold}` `{/bold}` | Start/stop bold |
| `{lns}` | Next line with no extra space |
| `{divider}` | Next line and draw the flavor text bar |

## 5. Art tab

- Upload by **drag and drop**, **File Upload**, or **Via URL**; **Paste from clipboard**
- **Enter a card name** to pull art, a dropdown to **select a specific card art**, and an **artist credit** field
- **Position/scale:** X, Y, Zoom (Scale) and Rotation (0 to 360) fields; you can also click and drag on the card to move the art, hold Shift to scale, or hold Ctrl to rotate
- **Auto Fit Art** button, **Autofit when setting art** checkbox, **Preserve art position** checkbox
- **Grayscale** toggle
- **Remove Art** button
- **Guidelines** toggle (shows text, art, watermark and set symbol bounds)

## 6. Set Symbol tab

- Upload by drag and drop, **File Upload**, or **Via URL**
- Or enter a **set code** and **rarity** (free-text fields) to load a symbol
- **Source dropdown:** CardConjurer, Gatherer, Hexproof.io
- Set codes follow Scryfall's codes (three-character code printed at the lower left of recent cards)
- X, Y and Zoom fields; **Reset Set Symbol**; **Remove Set Symbol**
- **Click and drag to move the set symbol instead of the art** (Shift to zoom)
- **Lock set symbol code** and **Lock set symbol URL** (saved between reloads)

## 7. Watermark tab

- Upload by drag and drop, or choose a **lore-based watermark** from a dropdown: None; General (Planeswalker, Desparked Planeswalker, DCI Star, DCI Logo); Monocolors (W, U, B, R, G, Colorless); Mechanics (Foretell); Phyrexian/Mirrodin (Phyrexian, Mirran); Ravnica guilds (Azorius, Dimir, Rakdos, Gruul, Selesnya, Orzhov, Izzet, Golgari, Boros, Simic); Strixhaven schools (Silverquill, Prismari, Witherbloom, Lorehold, Quandrix); Echoverse; New Capenna families (Brokers, Obscura, Maestros, Riveteers, Cabaretti); Tarkir clans, old timeline (Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu, Temur) and new timeline (Ojutai, Silumgar, Kolaghan, Atarka, Dromoka); Theros poleis (Akros, Meletis, Setessa); Unstable factions (Order of the Widget, Agents of S.N.E.A.K., League of Dastardly Doom, Goblin Explosioneers, Crossbreed Labs); Avatar: The Last Airbender (Water Tribe, Earth Kingdom, Fire Nation, Air Nomads); Custom (Purple Mana)
- **Left and right watermark colors** (two dropdowns): None, Actual Image, White, Blue, Black, Red, Green, Gold, Artifact/Colorless, Land, True White, True Black; or choose colors manually
- **Opacity** field; X, Y and Scale fields; **Reset Watermark**; **Remove Watermark**

## 8. Collector tab (rarity, number, set, artist, serial number)

Text fields: **Number, Rarity, Note, Set, Language, Artist**, and a **Year** number box.

**Serial number** (such as 001/500): number and total fields, with X, Y and Scale placement, **Reset Serial Number Placement**, and **Toggle Star/Dot** (star appears on foils, dot on regular cards).

## 9. Import/Save tab and download

- **Import a real card by name**, with an option to include all unique prints and a dropdown to pick a specific print
- **Import language:** English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Phyrexian
- **Paste full text** and **Paste card**
- **Save Card**, **Load a saved card**, **Delete Card**
- **Download Cards File** (all saved cards), **Download All Images** (zip, can be slow), upload a previously downloaded cards file, **Delete All**
- Saved cards live in your browser's localstorage (about 5 MB). Uploading art by URL instead of from your computer saves a lot of space.
- **Download your card** button under the preview
- **Tutorial** tab with a basics video/guide
